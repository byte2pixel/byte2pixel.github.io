About

I am a senior developer at Emerson, where I mentor and pair with a team of more than 40 developers across the globe. My day-to-day is C# on the .NET backend and TypeScript, React, and Redux on the front end, building the next generation of valve configuration software for industrial customers.

My career started in publishing, writing COBOL transaction-processing programs and running database migrations for new clients. From there I moved into embedded AV control systems, then military mapping and mission-planning software, then a remote-desktop product that streams 3D applications from cloud workstations. Each move added a layer: low-level systems, client and server architecture, real-time video, and now large-scale product development.

Today my favorite place to work is the .NET ecosystem, especially ASP.NET Core and .NET Aspire for cloud applications. Outside of work I write open-source libraries and tools, publish Twitch extensions used by hundreds of streamers, and I am finishing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Full Sail University while working full time.
